Fresno City College Student Support Resources

A single starting point for academic, technology, and life/basic-needs support available to FCC students.

🟡 Start Here: Need Help With Food, Housing, or Basic Needs?

Before browsing the resources below, we strongly encourage every student who is struggling with food, housing, transportation, childcare, finances, or other basic needs to fill out the Basic Needs Student Support Request Form. A Basic Needs Counselor will personally reach out to connect you with the right help — often within 2 business days.

Fill Out the Basic Needs Support Request Form
Located in Student Center Room 216 · Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm · basicneeds@fresnocitycollege.edu · (559) 442-8299
Learn more on the Basic Needs Resources page.
Emergency? Call 911 or Campus Police at (559) 244-5911.
Need help after hours? Call United Way 2-1-1 — a free, confidential, multilingual helpline open 24/7 for food, shelter, and other services.
